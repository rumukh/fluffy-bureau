# Case 2 notes

## Fact check (T18, Q23)

1. `C2-9-01` — «Сороки не тащат всё блестящее. Учёные это проверили.»  
   Verified and kept. University of Exeter / ScienceDaily (2014) reports that tested magpies avoided unfamiliar shiny and non-shiny objects; Audubon summarizes the same result. Conclusion: the story must not confirm the thieving-magpie myth.

2. `C2-9-02` — PM original: «Почтовые голуби правда носили письма. Они всегда летят домой.»  
   Reliable sources describe homing pigeons as message carriers with strong home-navigation abilities, but “always” is too absolute. Changed to: «Почтовые голуби носили письма. Они умеют находить дом.»

3. `C2-9-03` — «Сороки строят гнёзда с крышей из веточек.»  
   Verified with Cornell Lab / All About Birds: Eurasian Magpies commonly build domed nests from sticks and twigs, though not every nest is domed. The child-facing line is acceptable as a simple general statement.

Sources checked by web search: ScienceDaily / University of Exeter 2014 magpie folklore report; Audubon summary of magpie shiny-object study; Cornell Lab All About Birds Eurasian Magpie life history; homing-pigeon navigation/message-carrying sources summarized from ScienceDaily / research sources.
