## Fact check (T18, Q23)

1. `C3-10-01` — fireflies: kept. Britannica explains firefly bioluminescence as “cold light” with almost all energy emitted as visible light and very little heat. Conclusion: child-facing wording “почти не греет” is supported.
2. `C3-10-02` — bats: softened from all bats to “Многие летучие мыши…”. Britannica / National Park Service-style summaries describe bat echolocation as high-frequency calls and listening to returning echoes; not every bat relies on it the same way. Conclusion: softened wording is safer.
3. `C3-10-03` — owls: kept. Audubon and peer-reviewed summaries of silent owl flight describe soft/velvety/fringed feathers reducing flight noise. Conclusion: “почти бесшумно” and “мягкие перья” are supported.
